The notes above say `homepage` changed to point at the project site. It did not:
`Cargo.toml` has `homepage = "https://github.com/pandasir/gander"` in both v0.6.4
and v0.6.5, and it has never pointed anywhere else. What changed is `keywords`
(now led by `htop`) and `gander-bin`'s `description`. `git diff v0.6.4..v0.6.5 --
Cargo.toml` shows the whole change.

This cannot be fixed in the tag itself. Moving a tag that shipped a working
release would make two different archives share a name, so the correction is
recorded here instead.
